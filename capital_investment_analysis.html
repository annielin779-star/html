How it is calculated

NPV = −Initial investment + Σ [Annual net cash inflow ÷ (1 + discount rate)year].

Profitability index = Present value of all future cash inflows ÷ Initial investment.

Simple payback = Initial investment ÷ Annual net cash inflow (when annual inflows are positive).

Assumes the same net cash inflow occurs at the end of every year, with no salvage value or additional investment. This educational calculator does not account for taxes, inflation, or uncertainty unless already reflected in your inputs.
