How to Interpret the Results

NPV = sum of discounted future cash inflows − initial investment. A positive NPV suggests the project earns more than the required discount rate. Profitability Index = present value of inflows ÷ initial investment; a value greater than 1 is favorable. Simple Payback Period = initial investment ÷ annual inflow, ignoring the time value of money. Payback beyond the project life is labeled as not recovered within the project.

This calculator is an educational model, not a complete investment recommendation. It assumes the same inflow every year and excludes taxes, risk adjustments, and residual value.
